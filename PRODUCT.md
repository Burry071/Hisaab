# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Pakistani individuals and freelancers managing finances across multiple disjointed institutions (wallets and banks). They face fragmented financial data where banks don't talk to each other. Their job to be done is to track their net balance securely, resolve contradictions in transaction data, receive actionable budgeting insights, and predict future bills without sacrificing their privacy.

## Product Purpose
Hisaab is Pakistan's first AI-native financial intelligence app. It ingests financial signals (SMS, notifications, PDFs) entirely on-device, detects contradictions, and leverages an Antigravity agent pipeline to generate predictive insights and simulate financial actions. It aims to empower users by telling them what is coming, not just what happened, while acting as a privacy-first, trustworthy agent.

## Brand Personality
Refined, intelligent, trusted, confident, calm, and authoritative.

## Anti-references
- Bright, playful, noisy SaaS styles
- Overstimulating dashboards with excessive, identical cards
- "Black box" AI that hides its reasoning
- Cliché fintech templates with huge gradient text numbers

## Design Principles
- **Refined Dark Intelligence:** Use a premium dark-first design language that makes financial data feel secure and trusted, not stressful.
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Always expose the AI's reasoning (Agent Trace View). Prove it isn't a black box.
- **Privacy-First Trust:** Make it clear that raw data never leaves the device.
- **Actionable Clarity:** Insights must be predictive and actionable, not just a summary of past behavior. Provide before/after simulations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for readability in a dark mode setting.
- Multi-lingual support (Urdu via Noto Nastaliq Urdu) alongside English.
- Clear error states and conflict banners (using amber/red) with high legibility.
- Support for readable monospace fonts for technical data (Agent Traces and transaction IDs).
